Interactive Web-GIS for mapping abandoned paddy lands and reported / suspected land filling

About PaddyWatch

PaddyWatch is an interactive Web-GIS application developed to visualize the spatial distribution of abandoned paddy lands and reported or suspected land filling in the Western Province of Sri Lanka.

The application combines spatial datasets related to agricultural land, population, transportation, administrative boundaries and environmental features to provide a simple platform for spatial exploration and community engagement.

Main objectives

  • Visualize the distribution of abandoned paddy lands.
  • Calculate and display paddy land abandonment rates.
  • Identify reported or suspected land-filling locations.
  • Provide supporting spatial information for interpretation.
  • Allow the public to report locations related to the topic.

How to use the Web Map

  1. Open the Interactive Map tab.
  2. Select a base map such as OpenStreetMap or satellite imagery.
  3. Turn thematic layers on or off.
  4. Click a grid cell to view its attributes.
  5. Use the report section to share information about a location.

Data & Methodology

The Web-GIS combines spatial datasets prepared using GIS software and published through GitHub Pages.

Dataset Purpose
Abandoned Paddy Lands Main thematic layer showing abandoned paddy extent.
Paddy Lands Used to calculate the abandonment rate.
Population Density 2024 Provides demographic context.
A-Class Roads Transport accessibility context.
B-Class Roads Transport accessibility context.
Expressways Major transport infrastructure context.
Western Province Study area boundary.
Districts Administrative context.
DSD Boundaries Administrative context.
Rivers Environmental and hydrological context.

Abandonment Rate

The abandonment rate is calculated dynamically within the Web-GIS:

Abandonment Rate (%) = (Abandoned Paddy Area / Total Paddy Area) × 100

This indicator is calculated when the user interacts with the map rather than being stored as a separate GIS attribute.
