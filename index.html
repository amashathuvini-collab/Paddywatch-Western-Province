PaddyWatch

Western Province, Sri Lanka

Mapping abandoned paddy lands and suspected illegal land filling

About this Web-GIS

PaddyWatch is an interactive Web-GIS application designed to explore abandoned paddy lands and reported land filling in the Western Province of Sri Lanka.

The map brings together spatial data and community observations to support land monitoring and planning.

Objectives

  • Map abandoned paddy land locations.
  • Display reported or suspected land filling.
  • Encourage anonymous community observations.
  • Support evidence-based planning decisions.

Data & Methodology

Data sources

  • Abandoned paddy land layer: study data.
  • Land filling layer: study data / reports.
  • Study boundary: administrative GIS data.
  • Basemaps: OpenStreetMap and Esri imagery.
  • Community observations: Google Forms.

Method

Spatial layers are displayed using Leaflet. Users can explore mapped features and submit community observations through a separate form.

Limitations

Community reports are not proof of illegal activity. Location accuracy, data completeness and verification depend on the available evidence.

PL 3508 – Advanced GIS and Remote Sensing for Planning

Data should be interpreted according to its source, date, accuracy and verification status.
