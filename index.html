PaddyWatch – Western Province

Interactive Web-GIS application for exploring abandoned paddy lands, paddy land distribution, population density, GND population and reported/suspected land filling.

🌾 Abandoned Paddy Lands

Abandoned paddy land is aggregated from the available grid data to calculate a DSD-wise abandonment rate.

DSD Abandonment Rate (%) = Σ Abandoned Paddy Area ÷ Σ Paddy Area × 100

⚠ Land Filling

Reported and suspected land-filling locations are displayed separately as point locations.

👥 Population 2024

The GND-level population_2024.geojson is used for GND population information and GND administrative boundaries.

📊 Population Density 2024

The 1000 m population-density grid is shown using a graduated yellow-to-orange colour ramp.

DSD-wise Abandonment Rate

The abandoned paddy dataset is grid-based. Therefore, the application first identifies the DSD containing each abandoned grid.

The abandoned paddy areas of all grids within each DSD are summed.

The paddy areas of the corresponding paddy grid cells within each DSD are also summed.

DSD Abandonment Rate (%) = Σ Abandoned Paddy Area ÷ Σ Paddy Area × 100

The final percentage is displayed using a green sequential colour ramp.

Limitations

  • The GND population layer is also used to represent GND boundaries.
  • Demo land-filling points are only for testing and presentation.
  • DSD-wise abandonment results depend on the spatial correspondence of the grid and DSD datasets.
