Web-GIS Application for Mapping Abandoned Paddy Lands and Illegal Land Filling in Western Province, Sri Lanka

PaddyWatch – Western Province

A Web-GIS application developed to visualize abandoned paddy lands, paddy land distribution, population density and reported land-filling locations in Western Province.

The application supports spatial visualization, administrative exploration and community reporting.

🌾 Abandoned Paddy Lands

The map calculates the abandonment rate using abandoned paddy area and total paddy area.

Abandonment Rate (%) = Abandoned Paddy Area ÷ Total Paddy Area × 100

⚠ Reported / Suspected Land Filling

Locations reported by the community can be visualized separately from the paddy-land analysis.

👥 Population 2024

The application contains a 2024 population-density grid and a separate 2024 GND-level population layer.

Community Engagement

Community participation can provide local information about abandoned paddy lands and suspected land filling.

Information collected through the reporting form

  1. Reporting type
  2. Nearest village, road or landmark
  3. GN Division, if known
  4. Date of observation
  5. Description of the observation
  6. Duration of land being unused or filled
  7. Optional latitude and longitude
  8. Optional photo evidence

Submitted responses can be connected to a Google Sheets response sheet for further analysis.

Abandonment Rate Calculation

The abandonment rate is calculated for each grid using the abandoned paddy area and total paddy area.

Abandonment Rate (%) = (Abandoned Paddy Area / Total Paddy Area) × 100

The resulting percentage is represented using a green sequential colour ramp.

Limitations

  • GND boundaries are represented using the population_2024.geojson dataset rather than the large standalone GND boundary file.
  • Community-reported locations require verification before being treated as confirmed land-filling sites.
  • The abandonment rate depends on the spatial correspondence between the paddy and abandoned-paddy datasets.
