Abandonment Rate Method

Because the abandoned-paddy information is DSD-wise, the application first identifies the DSD of each paddy grid. For each DSD, the DSD-level abandoned paddy amount is divided by the number of paddy grids in that DSD. The resulting estimated abandoned amount per grid is then compared with the paddy area of that grid.

Estimated abandoned area per grid = DSD abandoned paddy area ÷ number of paddy grids in DSD

Estimated abandonment rate (%) = estimated abandoned area per grid ÷ paddy area of grid × 100

This is an allocation-based indicator, not a direct field measurement of abandonment for each grid.
