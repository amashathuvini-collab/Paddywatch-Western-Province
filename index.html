PaddyWatch – Western Province

Western Province is a highly urbanised region where agricultural land is under increasing pressure from settlement growth, infrastructure development and land-use change. Paddy lands are important for food production, drainage and the environmental functions of low-lying areas.

PaddyWatch brings together information on paddy-land abandonment, paddy-land distribution, 2024 population patterns, administrative areas, transport networks and reported or suspected land filling. The map is designed to help users understand where these pressures occur and how they are spatially related.

🌾 Paddy Land Abandonment

Abandoned paddy information available at DSD level is distributed across the corresponding paddy grids and compared with the paddy area of each grid to estimate an abandonment rate.

🏘 Population 2024

The 2024 population-density grid and GND population information provide a spatial view of population distribution and settlement pressure.

⚠ Land Filling

Reported or suspected land-filling locations are shown separately so that potential agricultural-land conversion can be viewed alongside the wider spatial pattern.

Abandonment Rate Method

The abandoned-paddy amount is DSD-level and is repeated across the grids belonging to that DSD. The application uses one DSD total, counts the grids carrying that DSD value, divides the DSD total by that grid count, and assigns the estimated amount to each grid. The result is compared with the paddy area of that same grid.

Estimated abandoned area per grid = DSD abandoned paddy amount ÷ number of paddy grids in DSD

Estimated abandonment rate (%) = estimated abandoned area per grid ÷ paddy area of the same grid × 100
